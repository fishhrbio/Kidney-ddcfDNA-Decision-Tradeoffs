A working computational framework is the beginning. Matched measurements and independent validation are what would support a new biological finding.

Proposed study / no molecular results yet

The focused research question

Do prespecified biopsy-expression associations reproduce when fractional–absolute discordance is evaluated under explicit classification uncertainty?

A discordance label is calculated from the two measurements. It does not contain information beyond those inputs; its value would be as a useful, reproducible summary of their relationship.

The complete framework

  1. Implemented

    Reconstruct published diagnostic tradeoffs

    Unique integer counts reproduce the four rules and all 16 reported rates. These are secondary analyses of published summaries.

  2. Implemented / hypothetical

    Quantify classification stability

    Exact bounded-state calculations retain shared fraction error. A fixed synthetic grid compares attainable states with an independent-error envelope.

  3. Tools prepared / data needed

    Test prespecified molecular associations

    Patient-separated module scoring and a minimal association estimator are included. Externally defined gene sets, paired blood/biopsy data and a finalized adjusted model are still required.

  4. Not yet performed

    Validate before making a stronger claim

    Freeze the protocol, assess precision, keep repeated patients together and test in independent data. Neither synthetic stability nor an internal split demonstrates improved clinical outcomes.

The current data checkpoint

The inspected standard metadata for GSE305222 describe 604 biopsy-expression samples, but do not supply matched numeric fraction/concentration measurements or patient identifiers. Supplements or authorized investigator data may resolve that gap.

Pair correctly

Verify sample IDs, assay units, blood/biopsy timing, reference labels and patient relationships.

Specify before testing

Lock module sources, measurement assumptions, covariates, contrasts and multiplicity handling.

Replicate honestly

Report uncertainty and null results. Distinguish internal assessment from independent external validation.

What is—and is not—new

Combined dd-cfDNA measurements, denominator effects and molecular injury associations have established precedents. The narrower candidate contribution integrates attainable-state stability, explicit uncertainty and prespecified molecular validation. Publication-level novelty remains provisional.

Closest primary work: combined fraction/quantity rules, molecular injury processes, and validated composite scores.
